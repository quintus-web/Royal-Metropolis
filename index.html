About the academy

A school built on the shape of its land.

Nyamira's ridges are terraced for tea; Royal Metropolis Academy is terraced for learning — each level built deliberately on the one below it, so no learner is asked to reach for a rung that isn't there yet.

Founded in 2011 on a four-acre ridge-top site outside Nyamira town, Royal Metropolis Academy began with two classrooms and forty learners. Today it serves close to 900 children from Pre-Primary through Grade 9, drawing families from across Nyamira, Kisii, and Bomet counties who want a school small enough to know every child's name and structured enough to prepare them for national assessment and beyond.

We teach the Kenyan Competency-Based Curriculum in full, with an emphasis on the things a highlands upbringing already gives a child a head start on: agriculture and environmental science, practical craft and technical skills, and a discipline for early mornings.

Vision

To raise learners from the Nyamira highlands who lead with competence, conscience, and confidence — at home and beyond it.

Mission

To deliver the CBC curriculum with rigour and warmth, in a boarding and day environment that is safe, well-resourced, and rooted in the values of the community it serves.

The CBC pathway

Every level, built on the one before.

We follow Kenya's Competency-Based Curriculum from Pre-Primary through Junior School, with dedicated teachers and facilities at each stage of the ladder.

01

Pre-Primary

PP1 – PP2 · Ages 4–5

Play-based learning in literacy, numeracy, and motor skills, in classrooms built around a fenced outdoor learning garden.

02

Lower Primary

Grade 1 – 3 · Ages 6–8

Foundational literacy and numeracy alongside creative arts, environmental activities, and the start of Kiswahili and English fluency.

03

Upper Primary

Grade 4 – 6 · Ages 9–11

Subject specialisation begins: science, social studies, agriculture, and pre-technical studies taught by dedicated subject teachers.

04

Junior School

Grade 7 – 9 · Ages 12–14

Learners choose from optional pathway subjects — including agriculture, computer science, and pre-technical studies — ahead of Senior School placement.

Campus life

Life on the ridge, beyond the classroom.

A working campus with boarding houses, sports fields, and a full timetable of clubs — set among the school's own tea and vegetable gardens.

Boarding houses

Four boarding houses named for Nyamira's own hills and rivers, each with a resident house-parent and a 1:12 supervision ratio.

Sports & athletics

Football, netball, volleyball, and cross-country on our own grounds, plus an annual inter-house athletics meet on the highland track.

Library & ICT hub

A 6,000-volume library and a 40-station computer lab, open to boarders every evening for prep and independent reading.

School farm & gardens

A working tea plot, vegetable garden, and dairy unit that supply the dining hall and anchor our agriculture curriculum.

Clubs & societies

Drama, debate, environmental club, Young Farmers, and a chess team that has taken the sub-county trophy three years running.

Transport & day scholars

Four school bus routes covering Nyamira town, Ekerenyo, and Manga, for day scholars arriving before 7:30am.

Admissions

How to join Royal Metropolis Academy.

We admit new learners at the start of every term, subject to space at each grade level. Here's what the process looks like from first enquiry to first day.

  1. Enquire

    Call, email, or visit the admissions office to check space at your child's grade level.

  2. Visit & assess

    Tour the campus and sit a short placement assessment in literacy and numeracy for the appropriate grade.

  3. Apply

    Submit the application form with the learner's birth certificate, latest report form, and two passport photos.

  4. Confirm & enroll

    Pay the admission fee to secure the place, collect the uniform and boarding list, and begin on the agreed date.

What you'll need

Have these ready before your visit to move through admissions in a single trip.

  • Learner's birth certificate or NEMIS number
  • Latest school report / transition letter
  • Two passport-size photographs
  • Immunisation card (Pre-Primary & Lower Primary)
  • Copy of parent/guardian national ID
